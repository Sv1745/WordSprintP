How to Play & Rules

Game Objective

  • Guess the 5-letter secret word within your allowed attempt limit (default: 5 attempts).
  • Each guess must be a valid 5-letter English word.
  • Press ENTER (or click Submit Word) to submit your guess.

What Each Color Means

GREEN
Correct Spot: Letter is in the word and in the exact right position.
YELLOW
Wrong Spot: Letter is in the word, but in a different position.
GRAY
Not In Word: Letter is not present anywhere in the secret word.

Visual Examples

W E A R Y

W is Green (correct spot).

P I L O T

I is Yellow (wrong spot).

V A G U E
